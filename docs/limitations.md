# Limitations

- Public ASA Goals Added measures measurable on-ball impact, not every form of player contribution.
- The model does not estimate adjusted team impact, off-ball contribution, or plus-minus.
- Guaranteed compensation ≠ Salary Budget Charge, acquisition cost, or total contract value.
- 2026 is partial-season; low-minute players are uncertain.
- Position labels can be noisy for hybrid roles.
- Goalkeepers excluded in v1.1.0.
- No transfer-market clearing prices.
- Historical checks measure stability, not proven breakout prediction.
- Mid-season transfers can leave messy historical team identifiers (production identity prefers 2026 team).
