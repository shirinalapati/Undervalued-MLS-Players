# Case Study 2: Possession-oriented club

## Objective

Identify midfielders (and progressive defenders) who retain under pressure, progress
cleanly, and create without relying primarily on transition chaos.

## Example profile priors

- High `possession_weight` / `progression_weight`
- Immediate contribution may outweigh pure upside
- Budget medium–high

Illustrative selectable clubs in the dashboard: Columbus Crew, Inter Miami, Seattle Sounders, NYC FC.

## Role focus

- Primary: Ball-winning midfielder (as a possession-secure destroyer/progressor hybrid in MVP)
- Extension roles (future): Possession controller, Deep progressor, Chance-creating AM

## Filters

- Prefer higher pass completion / progressive pass percentiles
- Cost tier up to High if immediate impact priority is on
- Age 24–30 acceptable for starter-level need

## Reproduce

Shiny: Club **Columbus Crew** → Role **Ball-Winning Midfielder** → Priority **Immediate contributor**.

## Portfolio narrative

Contrast the shortlist against Case Study 1: the same database should reorder toward
retention/progression traits when the club archetype changes — proving the platform is
**club-configurable**, not a single Red Bulls model.
