# Case Study 3: Budget-conscious MLS club

## Objective

Maximize expected sporting contribution per estimated acquisition/salary cost.
Undervalued ≠ cheapest; surplus must remain positive after role fit and feasibility.

## Example profile priors

- Elevated `financial_value_weight`
- Lower `budget_tier`
- Open to USL / NEXT Pro / depth MLS trades

Illustrative clubs: Colorado Rapids, San Jose Earthquakes, CF Montréal.

## Role focus

Any MVP role, with hard cap on cost tier (Low–Moderate) and strong Financial Value score.

## Filters

- `maximum_cost_tier <= 2`
- Feasibility ≥ Realistic band
- Risk not extreme
- Prefer domestic when international slots are scarce (user toggle)

## Reproduce

Shiny: Club **Colorado Rapids** → Role **Transition Winger** → Max cost **Moderate** →
watch Financial Value and Feasibility columns dominate the sort via club conditioning.

## Portfolio narrative

Show 2–3 names that elite “global rank” lists would ignore (lower league, modest salary)
but who clear minutes thresholds and translate reasonably under MVP priors.
